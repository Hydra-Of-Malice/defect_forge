# Past Meta / Llama Hackathon and Program Winners (2023–2026), and Why They Won

Scope note: the research ran on Nov 3, 2026, with about 20 search and fetch calls. Primary sources are Meta blog and newsroom pages (ai.meta.com, about.fb.com) and organizer recaps (lablab.ai). Some items come only from search-result snippets or mirrors and are marked **[UNVERIFIED]**. Devpost, GitHub and YouTube links for individual winners were generally not found (see Gaps).

## Q1. Which Meta/Llama hackathons have happened, and who won?

### Takeaway
At least six Meta-run or Meta-sponsored Llama hackathons with published winners took place between May 2024 and mid-2025. Two kinds of event rewarded different things. Developer events (Llama 3 SF, LlamaCon, Llama 4 NYC) rewarded technically ambitious, often multimodal, vision or hardware, or agentic builds. "Impact" events (London, SF/online, São Paulo, Rome, LATAM) rewarded social-good apps in health, public services, education and accessibility. No winners have been announced for any 2026 Muse-era Meta event, and no SAM-focused or Ray-Ban/wearables hackathon with published winners was found.

### Inferences
- Meta's own developer hackathons (A, F, G), which are closest in spirit to the **Indie track**, gave 1st–3rd place to: a hardware and vision wearable, multi-agent systems, safety and interpretability research, an enterprise knowledge-graph agent with digital twins, fintech fraud detection with voice, a zero-fine-tune video-understanding system, and a real-time multimodal coach. These are technically ambitious, demo-heavy, and often multimodal or agentic projects. "Nice chatbot" projects did not place.
- In Impact-branded events (B–E), all top-3 placements were in health, public services, education or accessibility, and many were multilingual or for low-resource users.
- **Physical-world and robotics builds placed high whenever they appeared**: OpenGlass took 1st at Llama 3 SF and Gripmind 2nd in London. This is a good sign for the "Push the Frontier" theme (robotics and physical systems).
- **Winners used the newest model's headline capability**: Llama 3.2 Vision for Gripmind, Llama 3.2 90B multilingual for all Brazil winners, Llama 4 long context and multimodality for Geo-ML, Llama 4 image understanding for CCTV. By analogy, judges in 2026 will likely reward obvious use of what is new in Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, Muse Image and SAM 3.1.
- **Fine-tuning was not needed to win**. The Brazil winners and the CCTV Operator explicitly used pre-trained models via API, while the fine-tuning team (Concierge) did not place. Within a 24–48 hour build, a working demo beat model-training effort.
- **Domain-expert builders do well**. Geo-ML was built by a working geologist, a direct analog for "Build for Your World."
- Sponsor "Best use of X" prizes stacked on top of main-track wins (Llama Navigator AI won 5+ awards). Integrating sponsor tools can raise a project's prize total.

- Devpost, GitHub and YouTube links for individual winners were not found. Meta's recaps don't link them. Searching each project name plus "GitHub" or "Devpost" would be the next step.
- Per-place prize amounts for London and LlamaCon were not published.

- The Llama 4 NYC results rest on an unverified Reddit mirror.

## Q2. What did the Llama Impact Grants and the Llama Impact Accelerator fund, and what did Meta showcase as exemplary?

### Takeaway
Every grant and accelerator dollar found went to social-impact, often production-scale deployments. These cluster in healthcare, education, agriculture and public services, with multilingual, offline and low-connectivity delivery and messaging-app channels (WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS). This is the clearest public signal of what Meta treats as an "exemplary" Llama use case. It maps best to the **Startup track's** "holds up in production" criterion.

### Inferences
- **Healthcare is the single most-funded vertical.** It covers Jacaranda, HelpMum, Doses AI, MARMAR, Purple Labs, Kajou, DPE, Neural Labs, Four Minute Medicine and SamaCoach, plus hackathon winners Guardian and Pharmallama. **Education** (FoondaMate, Twiga, Nova Escola, Auckland, DAWN, CatalyzU) and **agriculture** (Digital Green, phosoAI, eFama, Solo Tech) come next.
- Recurring delivery traits: **multilingual or low-resource languages**, **offline or low-connectivity**, and **messaging-app channels** (WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS). These match Meta's own distribution platforms, so a build that runs inside WhatsApp or Messenger may resonate with Meta judges. This is inference, not stated by Meta.
- Several funded projects fit the 2026 "Push the Frontier" theme: Doses AI (robotic dispensing), Padova (molecule generation), Nayana (multimodal document vision), BluEye (disaster and climate).
- Grant and accelerator winners show **real users at scale** (e.g. FoondaMate's 4M students). That is a stronger fit for the Startup track's "holds up in production" criterion than hackathon demos are.

## Q4. Did any winning patterns repeat?

### Takeaway
Yes. Across about 30 placed hackathon projects and about 25 funded orgs, six patterns repeat: (1) healthcare, education and public services; (2) multimodal and vision; (3) voice interfaces; (4) multilingual or low-resource reach; (5) physical or hardware builds placing near the top; (6) on-device or offline privacy. Pure developer-productivity or "chat with your docs" apps rarely placed 1st.

### Cited Findings (tally; each item is sourced in Q1–Q2)
- **Multimodal and vision in top-3 placements:** OpenGlass (1st, [Meta](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-3-hackathon-recap-cerebral-valley/)), Gripmind (2nd, Llama 3.2 Vision, [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/11/metas-llama-impact-hackathon-pioneering-ai-solutions-for-public-good/)), Compliance Wizards (2nd, multimodal), Llama CCTV Operator (3rd, video), Geo-ML (Best API, text and image) ([Meta](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llamacon-hackathon/)), Pitch Perfect (1st NYC, body language and eye contact, [unverified](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/u/MetaforDevelopers)).
- **Voice:** Compliance Wizards (voice assistant), Gripmind (voice control), Curador.AI (voice guide, [Meta BR](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2024/11/primeiro-llama-impact-hackathon-no-brasil-reforca-a-importancia-do-codigo-aberto-para-ia)), Clara (voice-agent check-ins, [lablab](https://lablab.ai/blog/llama-impact-hackathon-sf-summary)), phosoAI (voice commands, [TechEconomy](https://techeconomy.ng/meta-unveils-recipients-of-inaugural-llama-impact-grants)), Pitch Perfect (voice analysis).

### Inferences
- **Event branding predicts the winning angle.** In "Impact" events, social good beat commercial projects every time. In open developer events (Llama 3 SF, LlamaCon), commercial and enterprise projects (OrgLens, Compliance Wizards) and research-ambitious projects (Team AAA, Mongoose Miner) won. The 2026 hackathon is a developer event with a $1M pool and a Startup track, so a commercial angle is likely viable, especially in Startup. A social-impact framing may still help on the "Build for Your World" theme.
- **Polish level:** all hackathon winners were 24–48 hour builds with live demos. Several used pre-trained models via API with no fine-tuning (Brazil winners, CCTV Operator). Winning was about a working, visible demo plus a clear user story, not infrastructure depth. The 2026 Indie criterion "whether it works" fits this. The 2026 Startup criterion "holds up in production" fits the grant and accelerator profile: real users, deployment channels such as WhatsApp, offline robustness.
- **Interaction-mode mix of top-3 winners:** vision (camera, video, glasses) is the most frequent differentiator, with voice second. Very few 1st-place winners were text-only. Llama Navigator AI (Chrome extension) and OrgLens are exceptions, and OrgLens differentiated through knowledge graphs and digital twins.
- **Mapping to 2026 models:** SAM 3.1 (segmentation and tracking in video) and Muse multimodal models directly enable the vision-heavy patterns (CCTV-style event detection, glasses or robotics perception, 3D or subsurface reconstruction à la Geo-ML). This is an inference from past winners, not evidence about 2026 judging.

### Gaps
- No quantitative judging data exists, so these patterns rest on placement only, not scores.
- Sample sizes per event are small (3–6 placed projects).

## Q5. What did the winners of 2026 Meta events look like (closest analog)?

### Takeaway
No winners of any 2026 Meta or Muse-era hackathon could be found. The current Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon is the first of its kind, and its winners are due around Nov 9, 2026. Entrants can build on the Meta Model API, Muse Code or Meta open-weights models. Submissions close Nov 4 and winners are to be announced at a live final on Nov 9, 2026, per a secondary blog that has not been checked against an official page. — [madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/03/meta-global-ai-developer-hackathon-1-million-prizes-muse-model-api/)
